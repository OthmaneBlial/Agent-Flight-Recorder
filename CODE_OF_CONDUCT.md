# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Agent Flight Recorder to be safe, respectful, and technically constructive. Be considerate, assume good faith, disagree with ideas rather than people, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or publishing another person's captured agent evidence is not acceptable in project spaces.

## Privacy in project discussions

Recorder traces can contain source code, prompts, paths, and operational metadata. Remove private material before attaching logs or bundles to an issue. Security-sensitive evidence belongs in a private vulnerability report, not a public thread.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe and productive project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or security-reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing the project.
